Hisar Coding Summit was created to grow our students' potential through digital transformation. This year it is held for the 13th time at Hisar Schools — in English and Turkish, taught by FabLab students to anyone who wants to learn.

Idealab, Hisar's FabLab, has been the heartbeat of innovation for over a decade: a maker space where creativity and collaboration turn ideas into real things.
